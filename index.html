⚙️ Settings

Raw material rates, labour wages and the processing cost used to calculate every batch.

Raw material rates (₹ per unit)
MaterialUnitRate

Change a material's unit anytime — it saves right away.

Labour & processing cost

Used for any product that doesn’t have its own fallback cost below. Auto-synced to the breakdown’s ₹/MT rate further down once that’s set up (only kicks in as a flat fallback when no budgeted monthly output is entered).

Fallback processing cost by product (₹/batch)

Different products don’t cost the same to process even on the same machinery. Pick a product to set its own fallback cost — it overrides the default above (and the breakdown’s ₹/MT rate) for that product’s batches. Leave a product without one and it keeps using the default.

Labour cost by product (₹/batch)

Some products take more labour to handle than others. Pick a product to set its own flat labour cost — it replaces the operator/loadman-count calculation above for that product’s batches. Leave a product without one and it keeps using the daily-wage calculation.

Labour cost by company (₹/MT)

A job work client’s contract can mean different labour handling too. Pick a company to set its own labour rate per metric tonne of output — it’s multiplied by each batch’s output and overrides both the daily-wage calculation and any product override above for batches tagged to that company. Leave a company without one and it keeps using whichever of those applies.

Processing cost breakdown (₹ per month)

Enter each monthly expense once. Divided by your budgeted monthly output, this gives a ₹/MT processing-cost rate that’s applied to every batch based on its own MT output. Saving here also copies this rate into the “Fallback processing cost per batch” field above, so the two always match.
